隱私權政策

介紹 隱私權 支援

本政策說明 GO IV 計算（以下稱「本 App」）如何處理您的資訊。使用本 App 即表示您理解下列內容。

IV 計算在您的裝置上進行

IV 與等級的推算在您的 iPhone 或 iPad 上完成，不會將您的截圖或計算輸出上傳到開發者的伺服器以進行 IV 演算。

截圖與暫存記憶體

您選擇的精靈摘要截圖僅用於當次上傳與辨識流程，處理期間保留在裝置記憶體中；離開該流程或關閉相關畫面後即釋放，本 App 不會將照片存入相簿或長期儲存空間。

歷史紀錄

本機歷史僅保存辨識與計算結果（例如種類、CP、HP、IV 組合等），不保存摘要截圖影像。

圖鑑資料（Firebase Storage）

物種名稱與三圍等數值 JSON會從 Firebase Storage（bucket：goiv-app.firebasestorage.app）下載以支援計算。該資料不含角色圖或官方立繪，本 App 也不從外部圖庫載入圖片。

當機報告（Firebase Crashlytics）

Firebase Crashlytics 只收集未捕捉的當機與除錯資訊，協助修復穩定性問題。不會上傳螢幕截圖，也不會將 CP、HP、種類寫進自訂紀錄。

廣告（Google AdMob）

IV 頁在「幫精靈評價」下方可能顯示原生廣告，結果頁底部可能顯示橫幅廣告。圖鑑第一層、圖鑑詳細頁與收藏第一層沒有廣告。App 會先透過 Apple 的 App Tracking Transparency 詢問追蹤許可；若您拒絕，廣告請求將以非個人化方式處理。

我們不出售個人資料

我們不會出售您的個人資料。

最後更新：2026-10-06。政策隨 App 功能調整時可能更新，請以本頁最新內容為準。

This policy describes how GO IV Calculator (“the app”) handles information. By using the app, you acknowledge the practices below.

IV calculation on your device

IV and level inference runs on your iPhone or iPad. Your screenshots and calculation results are not uploaded to the developer’s servers for IV computation.

Screenshots and memory

Summary screenshots you select are used only for the current upload and recognition flow, held in device memory during processing, and released when you leave that flow or dismiss the related screens. The app does not save photos to your photo library or long-term storage.

History

On-device history stores recognition and calculation results only (e.g., species, CP, HP, IV sets). It does not store summary screenshot images.

Species data (Firebase Storage)

Numeric JSON (names, base stats, etc.) is downloaded from Firebase Storage (bucket: goiv-app.firebasestorage.app) to power calculations. This data contains no character art or official artwork, and the app does not load images from an external image library.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

Firebase Crashlytics collects only uncaught crashes and debugging information to improve stability. Screenshots are not uploaded, and CP, HP, or species are not sent as custom log fields.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The IV tab may show a native ad under Appraise, and the results screen may show a banner at the bottom. The Dex list, Dex detail, and Saved list do not show ads. The app asks for tracking permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency; if you decline, ad requests are handled in a non-personalized manner.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information.

Last updated: 2026-10-06. This policy may change as app features evolve; refer to this page for the current version.
